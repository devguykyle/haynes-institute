# How to Read a Primary Source

A primary source should not be skimmed for a slogan. Read it as a whole document written by a real person in a real historical moment.

## Questions to Ask

- Who wrote this?
- When and where was it written?
- Who was supposed to hear or read it?
- What problem is the author trying to answer?
- What words need historical definition?
- What claims are theological, moral, political, or practical?
- What should a modern reader admire, question, or investigate further?
