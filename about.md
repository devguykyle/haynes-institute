# About

The Haynes Institute exists to recover and teach sources at the meeting place of theology, American history, and classical education.

The name honors Lemuel Haynes, an early Black American minister, Revolutionary War veteran, and writer whose life joined Christian conviction, learned preaching, and public courage.

The institute is especially concerned with helping and healing the Black American community through truth-telling, historical memory, moral formation, and careful reading.
